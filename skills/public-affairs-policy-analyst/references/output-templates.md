# Output Templates

Use only the template that fits the audience. Do not fill sections mechanically when a shorter answer is enough.

## 1. Rapid Policy Scan

**Bottom line:**  
One or two sentences with the decision-relevant judgment.

**What happened:**  
Exact event, institution, date, and status.

**Why it matters:**  
The concrete business-impact mechanism.

**What is uncertain:**  
Key unresolved facts or implementation questions.

**Watch next:**  
Two to four observable triggers.

---

## 2. One-Page Policy Brief

### Executive judgment
3–5 sentences. Lead with the main judgment, not background.

### What changed
- policy/event;
- institution;
- date;
- legal or political status.

### Business impact
Use the chain:
**policy → mechanism → business function → consequence**

### Stakeholder landscape
Only actors that can materially shape the issue.

### Scenarios
- Base case
- Upside / lower-pressure case
- Downside / higher-pressure case

Give triggers rather than arbitrary percentages unless reliable quantitative evidence exists.

### Recommended actions
Prioritized, actionable, and tied to the analysis.

### Evidence & confidence
List the strongest evidence, major uncertainty, and confidence level.

---

## 3. Deep Issue Analysis

### Question
State the public-affairs question in one sentence.

### Context
Only the background needed to understand the present issue.

### Institutional structure
Who decides, how, under what authority, and with what discretion?

### Competing interests and public values
What are the substantive points of conflict?

### Business intersection
Where exactly does the company's product, operations, ecosystem, or reputation intersect with the issue?

### Stakeholders
Map authority, interest, position, influence, and likely actions.

### Trajectory
What would move the issue toward or away from formal regulation or public controversy?

### Options
Compare the implications of different organizational responses.

### Recommendation
State the preferred course and why.

---

## 4. Stakeholder Map

| Stakeholder | Role | Verified position | Inferred interest | Influence | Likely next move | Engagement implication |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Add a note whenever “position” is unknown.

---

## 5. Public Communication Package

### Communication objective
What should the audience understand, believe, or do after the communication?

### Audience
Name the actual audience; do not write for “the public” as a single group.

### Core message
One sentence.

### Supporting points
Three or fewer, each tied to verifiable evidence.

### Proof points
Facts, data, practices, commitments, or third-party evidence.

### Sensitivities
Claims, phrases, or frames that could be misunderstood or contested.

### Q&A
For each difficult question:
- direct answer;
- evidence;
- boundary of what can responsibly be claimed.

### Escalation flags
Questions that require legal, policy, product, or leadership review before external use.
